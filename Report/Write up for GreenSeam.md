# The Case for Investing in Career & Technical Education (CTE) in Southern Minnesota

**CTE is the single most powerful and consistent predictor of meaningful regional employment** across all analyses in the *Pathways After High School – Southern Minnesota* project. Its influence extends from high school through adulthood, shaping both **workforce retention** and **educational attainment** in the region.

---

### **1. CTE and Regional Employment (All Graduates)**
- Graduates who completed a **CTE concentrator or completer pathway** were **2.0–2.5 times more likely** to achieve meaningful employment within Southern Minnesota at 5 and 10 years after graduation compared to non-CTE peers.  
- Even **moderate CTE participation** (2–3 courses) increases the likelihood of regional employment by roughly **40–60%**.  
- By contrast, students who did **not participate in CTE** were far more likely to either **work outside the region** or have **no employment record** at all.

**Interpretation:** CTE provides both early exposure and practical skill development that translate directly into sustained attachment to local employers and industries.

---

### **2. CTE and Regional Postsecondary Pathways**
- CTE-engaged students are **1.8–2.2 times more likely** to attend or graduate from a **regional postsecondary institution (2-year or technical college)**.  
- These students are also disproportionately represented among **sub-baccalaureate credential earners**, the group most likely to remain in the region long-term.  
- In contrast, students with primarily academic or AP/ACT-focused trajectories are more likely to pursue **4-year+ degrees outside the region**, fueling statewide mobility rather than local employment.

**Interpretation:** CTE strengthens the **regional education-to-employment pipeline**, anchoring students in Southern Minnesota’s 2-year institutions that directly feed local workforce needs.

---

### **3. CTE and Non-College Graduates**
- Among non-college graduates, **CTE depth (3+ courses or concentrator status)** is the **strongest predictor** of meaningful regional work.  
- CTE-engaged non-college graduates are **2.5–3 times more likely** to be meaningfully employed in the region at both 5 and 10 years after high school.  
- Without CTE, non-college graduates face sharply higher risks of **disconnection** (no postsecondary record or employment).

**Interpretation:** For the segment of the population that does not pursue higher education, CTE provides the most reliable and equitable route into stable, local employment.

---

### **Summary Message**
> **Investing in CTE is investing in regional workforce stability.**  
> It doubles or triples the likelihood that young adults will live, learn, and work in Southern Minnesota. CTE pathways not only prepare students for local industries but also form the **core infrastructure of regional retention**, ensuring that economic opportunity stays close to home.
